# Kalys: legal search for Kyrgyz law

Ask a question about the Constitution, Criminal Code, or Criminal Procedure Code
of the Kyrgyz Republic - in English, Kyrgyz, or Russian. Every answer is grounded
in the actual law and cited by exact article and edition date.

English text is a machine translation, made once from the official Russian text,
and is always labeled as such. The Kyrgyz and Russian originals are never changed.
